About Me

Cybersecurity Enthusiast & Developer

I am Ishwar Lal Khatri, a cybersecurity practitioner with a strong background in IT systems, hardware/software troubleshooting, and network fundamentals. My expertise covers offensive security, penetration testing, SOC operations, and vulnerability assessment, supported by a solid foundation in Computer Science and hands-on technical experience.

I have completed 150+ practical labs across TryHackMe, Hack The Box, and PortSwigger, gaining real-world skills in ethical hacking, malware analysis, Android exploitation, Windows security, and SOC activities such as log analysis, incident detection, threat hunting, and alert triage. I am proficient with a wide range of tools including Kali Linux, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Nmap, SQLMap, Wireshark, Hydra, and Hashcat, and I have experience managing operating systems, basic servers, network devices, and IT peripherals. I also develop custom Python tools such as a vulnerability scanner and a network sniffer.

I am committed to continuous learning across both cybersecurity and IT domains. By applying frameworks like OWASP Top 10, PTES, the Cyber Kill Chain, and SOC monitoring practices, I focus on identifying weaknesses, improving defenses, and contributing to secure, resilient digital environments.
